How this was put together

  • From HSBC and UOB Visa statements, CIMB savings statements, and Grab and Touch 'n Go screenshots.
  • Each purchase counts in the month it was made. Card bill payments and transfers between your own accounts are left out.
  • Large Touch 'n Go top-ups were traced to where the money went. Each card top-up costs a 1% fee.
  • The CIMB credit card has no statement, so each bill is one line.
  • September is missing the car loan and the ITWorld instalment, which were paid after the statements.
  • July starts 7 Jul (HSBC) and 24 Jul (UOB). October covers 1–7 Oct.
